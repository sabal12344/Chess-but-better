 Rules

                    

                

 
            


                

           
No checks and checkmates !! 

                    
                    

Game ends on following conditions
 
                    
    
                        
  • King runs out of lives (When king’s lifespan = 0) 
    • 
                        
  • King has no blue squares when its taken by the opposite king 
    • 
                    

                    
                    


                    
King’s life count decrements on following conditions

                    
    
                        
  • 
                         It gets captured by opponent’s piece or king 
    • 

                         
  • 
                    You run out of time  to make your move 
                
    • 
                     

                    
 
                   

                    
King’s life count increments on following conditions :-
 
                    
  • It (king) captures the opponent’s king
    • 
                        
  • 
                    Your pawn reaches the other end 

                    
                    
                    
What is blue square

                        
    
                          
  •  When a king captures king, the captured king teleports. 
                             But it can only teleport on the nearest first square from (a8 to h1)
                              where it cannot capture any of its opponent’s piece. Such square is called blue square.
                              If blue square does not exist,
                              then the captured king loses the game immediately
